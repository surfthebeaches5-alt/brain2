Phase 1 · Looking Back
The Audit
Before you step into who you're becoming, you need to see H1 clearly — the wins you actually earned, what you're outgrowing, and what needs to be left behind. Clear the clutter first.
1.1Every Small Win from H1

Add as many as you can. Big, small, trivial, profound. Every single one counts as evidence of who you already are.

1.2Identity Audit — What No Longer Fits

Be ruthlessly honest. The goal isn't to tear yourself down — it's to separate what you're still performing from who you're actually becoming. (The concrete people, spaces, and habits you're leaving belong in 1.3 below.)

1.3The Market Exit — What You're Leaving Behind

When a business rebrands, they exit markets that don't fit the new vision. You need to do the same — and be specific. Vague exits don't stick.

Ways of Working
Habits, work styles, routines that need to go
Relationships & Spaces
People, groups, environments to step back from
Patterns & Digital Habits
Mental loops, apps, and triggers to exit
My First Hard Boundary — and when I start it

Pick the one exit that matters most, be specific, and date it. Not "I'll set better limits" — but "I'm stepping back from ___, starting ___."
